About us

Pick it, cook it, enjoy it

Happy Noodle is a self-serve Korean ramyeon bar and art-toy cafe at Khao Yai. Choose your noodles and toppings, cook them yourself at our automatic noodle machines, and eat surrounded by hundreds of collectible figures.

How it works

Three easy steps

1

Choose & Pay

Pick your ramyeon and toppings, then pay at the cashier.

2

Cook at the machine

Tear the packet into the bowl and cook it at the automatic machine.

3

Add your egg last

Drop the egg in the last minute so it won't stick to the bottom.
